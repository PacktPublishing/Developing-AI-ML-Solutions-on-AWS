# Security and approval limits

Synthetic credit policy for the book's knowledge base. Invented for illustration.

## Minimum security

Every SME facility needs a personal guarantee from the promoter and guarantor
cheques signed by two signatories. Asset finance is also secured on the asset
financed.

## Approval limits

| Total exposure | Approved by |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,000,000 USD | Senior underwriter |
| Up to 5,000,000 USD | Head of Credit |
| Above 5,000,000 USD | Credit committee |

An amendment is approved at the level of the total exposure after the change.

## Single-obligor limit

Total exposure to one borrower and its related companies may not exceed
20,000,000 USD.
