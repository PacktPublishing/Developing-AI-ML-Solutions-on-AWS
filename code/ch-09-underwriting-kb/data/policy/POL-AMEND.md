# Amendments to existing facilities

Synthetic credit policy for the book's knowledge base. Invented for illustration.

## Principal moratorium and deferral

A principal moratorium of up to three months may be approved by Credit Policy
when the shortfall is temporary and has a named cause, such as delayed
receivables. Interest continues to accrue and is collected monthly during the
moratorium. A borrower more than 30 days past due is not eligible and is
referred to the recoveries team instead.

## Tenor extension

A tenor may be extended once, by up to six months. The extended schedule must
pass the DTI ceiling on current statements.

## Facility increase

An increase is assessed as a new request: fresh statements, a new DTI
calculation, and approval at the level the total exposure requires.

## Change of repayment account

A new direct debit mandate must be confirmed by Risk before the next repayment
date.
