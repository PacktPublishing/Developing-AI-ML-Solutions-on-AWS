# Debt-to-income assessment for SME borrowers

Synthetic credit policy for the book's knowledge base. Invented for illustration.

## How DTI is calculated

Debt-to-income (DTI) is the borrower's monthly debt service divided by average
monthly inflows. Debt service is the instalment on the proposed facility plus all
existing loan repayments. Average monthly inflows are total credits across the
bank statements provided, divided by the months they cover, after removing
transfers between the borrower's own accounts and one-off credits such as asset
sales or loan disbursements.

## Ceiling

The DTI ceiling is 40 percent for every sector. A request above the ceiling is
declined at underwriter level or reduced until it fits. Only the Head of Credit
may approve an exception, and the memo must state the reason.

## Statements required

At least three months of statements from every account the business trades
through. Grocery and provisions, agro supplies, and restaurant supply businesses
are seasonal, so six months are required to cover a full trading cycle, and the
underwriter uses the average of the weakest three months rather than the
average of all six.

## Existing repayments

Existing repayments are read from the statements, not taken from the
application form. A recurring debit to another lender counts even if the
borrower did not declare it.
